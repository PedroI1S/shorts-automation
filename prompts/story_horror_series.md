Write ONE original English first-person horror story told across exactly 3 or 4
Shorts, as requested. Plan the complete arc and its ending before writing Part 1.
This is a continuous story, not several standalone stories with the same props.

Keep one protagonist, the same narrator gender and voice, the same central threat,
consistent supernatural rules, chronology and character knowledge throughout.
Maintain a short continuity bible identifying the protagonist, setting, threat,
rules, planted clue and final payoff. Every part must change the situation and
give the viewer a meaningful discovery or consequence, without padding.

Part 1 starts with an immediate disturbing event and establishes the stakes.
Parts 2 onward open by answering the previous cliffhanger, with at most one short
line of context woven into the action. Each part also needs an understandable
opening for someone who encounters it first. Do not spend a Short on recap.

End EVERY nonfinal part at a concrete revelation, dangerous decision or immediate
consequence that naturally demands the next part. Do not cut a sentence in half,
withhold an obvious fact the narrator already knows, ask a vague rhetorical
question, or add an unrelated villain just to create a cliffhanger.

The final part resolves the central danger and pays off the planted clue. It may
leave an unsettling final implication, but must not advertise a nonexistent next
episode or replace the resolution with another unanswered cliffhanger.

There is no minimum word count or duration. Never pad. Each individual script
must fit the supplied per-Short word limit including any recap. Keep sentences
natural for spoken narration. No spoken part labels, subscribe requests or other
marketing: the renderer adds part labels and a continuation cue on screen, and
the uploader supplies searchable numbered titles. Do not claim a next part is
already public. No gore for shock value, stage directions, ellipses or em dashes.
Do not present fictional events as verified real events.

Privately check the entire arc: each cliffhanger must be answered in the next
part; nobody changes identity or gender; no character knows an unseen event;
actions are physically possible; the final payoff follows the established rules.
