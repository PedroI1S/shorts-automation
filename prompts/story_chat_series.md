Write ONE original English relationship-drama story told across exactly 3 or 4
Shorts, as requested. Each Short is one text-message thread shown on screen and
read aloud by each sender in their own voice. Plan the complete arc and its
ending before writing Part 1. This is one continuous story, not several chats
that happen to share names.

Genre: cheating, betrayal, secrets and satisfying revenge. The protagonist is
"me", the first-person phone owner. Keep the same protagonist, gender, contacts,
names, relationships, chronology and what each person knows throughout. Maintain
a short continuity bible naming the protagonist, each contact, the betrayal or
conflict, the planted evidence and the final payoff.

Every message is something a real person would text. The story must be fully
understandable from the messages alone: no narrator, no stage directions, no
"[photo]" placeholders. When someone sends evidence, describe it in their own
words. Messages are read aloud, so write complete, natural phrases: no emoji,
no hashtags, no text-speak or abbreviations that sound wrong spoken, no links,
handles, addresses or phone numbers. Short punchy messages are good; mix them
with longer ones where tension builds.

Part 1 opens with an immediately alarming message as the first line, so a
viewer understands the stakes within seconds. Parts 2 onward answer the
previous cliffhanger at once, with at most one line of context woven in. Each
part may switch to a different listed contact when the story needs it, but
every part is one conversation between me and exactly one contact.

End EVERY nonfinal part on a concrete revelation, ultimatum or dangerous
decision that naturally demands the next part. Do not end on a vague question,
a cut-off sentence, or a twist that contradicts earlier messages.

The final part resolves the central betrayal with a clear, satisfying payoff
built on the planted evidence. Revenge stays legal and nonviolent: exposure,
consequences, leaving, outsmarting. No threats of violence, stalking, doxxing,
self-harm or crimes presented as a solution.

Keep it advertiser-friendly: no explicit sexual content, no slurs, no graphic
violence, mild language at most. All characters are adults. Use invented first
names only; never real people, brands, apps or companies. Do not present the
story as real events.

There is no minimum word count or duration. Never pad. No spoken part labels,
subscribe requests or other marketing: the renderer adds part labels and a
continuation cue on screen, and the uploader supplies numbered titles.

Privately check the entire arc: each cliffhanger is answered in the next part;
nobody changes name or gender; nobody refers to a message they never saw;
every reveal follows from earlier evidence; the finale pays off the conflict.
