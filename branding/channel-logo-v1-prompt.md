## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: YouTube channel profile logo, square image, optimized for a circular avatar crop.
Primary request: Create one polished original logo for an English horror-story Shorts channel that pairs frightening narrated stories with Minecraft parkour footage. Make the brand feel suspenseful, mature, memorable, and immediately legible at a tiny profile size.
Subject: One bold, compact emblem: a stylized open book whose raised pages form the silhouette of a mysterious hooded storyteller. In the dark central negative space, two small sharp yellow eyes suggest an eerie presence. Make book and hidden face read as a single clever integrated mark, not separate clip-art elements.
Style/medium: Refined flat vector-like logo design with clean geometric shapes, strong silhouette, balanced negative space, and very few details. Slight angular geometry subtly connects to the block-based footage, without using Minecraft characters, assets, or branding.
Scene/backdrop: Solid near-black square background.
Color palette: Ivory mark, near-black negative space, and a restrained vivid yellow accent that relates to the channel's moving yellow caption highlight.
Composition/framing: Single centered emblem occupying about 68 percent of the canvas; generous clear margins; keep all meaningful details safely inside the central circular crop. Strong, deliberate proportions. No surrounding text or decorative badge ring.
Text: None. No letters, channel name, tagline, or watermark.
Constraints: Output only the finished logo artwork. No mockup, no presentation board, no multiple options, no border, no gradients, no 3D rendering, no texture, no gore, no tiny ornaments, no existing brand logos.
